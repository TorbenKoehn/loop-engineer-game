# R072: Review of T071

## Summary

Round 1. Adds the bot contract with `botRng` (a run-seed fork labelled by the decision point), the random and greedy bots, a headless `playRun` driver, the greedy heuristic table in testing.md, and tests. The diff is 362 production lines and 569 total (`npm run harness:diff`, within budget). Everything is staged and nothing is untracked.

- [x] AC1 verified: `npx vitest run tools/balance` passed 13 of 13. These include "same seed gives the same run, and replay rebuilds it" and "is pure" for both bots. The random bot gets its randomness only from `fork(setup.seed, ...)`, and the greedy bot is deterministic.
- [x] AC2 verified: the test "random bot completes 200 runs, each ending in runEnd" passes. `playRun` throws on an illegal action, an empty legal list, or more than 2000 actions.
- [x] AC3 verified: the heuristic is in testing.md and covered by 6 unit tests in greedy.test.ts, and the sanity test (boss reached in at least 100 of 200 runs) passes. I also ran my own sample with plain `node`, 100 runs per harness on unseen seeds rv-0..99:
  - greedy, Terminal Purist: boss 86, shipped 80
  - greedy, IDE Companion: boss 93, shipped 90
  - random: boss 0 and shipped 0 on both harnesses

  These match the reported rates. Speed was about 300 greedy runs/s.

Architecture: tools/balance imports only src/run, src/content and src/sim, and `tests/arch.test.ts` passes (5 of 5). Greedy win rates are far above the 35–65% exit target in vertical-slice.md. The task notes this as tuning input for T077, which is correct because tuning is not in T071's scope.
