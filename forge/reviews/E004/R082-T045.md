# R082: Review of T045

## Summary

Round 1. The new src/run/build/ adds moveTool, equip, unequip, swap and setPolicy. They are accepted in map, reward, shop and event, and mode and `pending` stay unchanged. The 80% limit uses the modified window and applies to equip, unequip and swap. The selectors are built on the sim's own `createCtx`/`baseline`/`breakpoints` over `loadoutInput`, and `combatInput` now spreads `loadoutInput`, so the UI and the fight share one input builder. The limit also holds on the other paths:
- `gain` equips only within the limit and otherwise puts the item in the stash.
- `sell` refuses with baselineOverLimit.
- `discardRefs(…, state)` leaves out removals that would break the limit.

Build actions stay out of `legalActions` and `buildActions(state)` lists them instead. That is fine: replay only folds `apply`, the only callers of `legalActions` are the sandbox and placeholder, and run-state.md documents the decision. In shop.test.ts the fixture only swaps fake skill ids for real ones, because `gain` now resolves content. The assertions are unchanged in meaning. The T068 orchestrator note is outside this task. Everything is staged. Diff: production=268, total=692. `npm run check` exit 0.
