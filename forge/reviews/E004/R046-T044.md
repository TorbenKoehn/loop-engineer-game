# R046: Review of T044

## Summary

Round 1. Adds `src/run/shop.ts`, which rolls the offers and the sale and handles buy, reroll, sell and leaveShop. Travelling to a registry node now opens the shop, and a discard pending can carry a `resume` shop. The raw diff is 593+/36- lines. Production is 265 added and 22 removed lines (287) across 8 files, under the 400-line budget. `npx vitest run src/run src/ui` gave 109 passed and `tsc --noEmit` is clean. `npm run check` passed with 0 lint errors; `src/run` at 13 files is only a warn_at warning. Everything is staged, with no unstaged or untracked files.

The numbers match economy.md exactly:
- Slot odds: tool 55/35/10 (x3), skill 60/30/10 and memory 50/35/15.
- Prices: 12/18/26, 14/20/28 and 18/26/34.
- Sale: `floor(price*70/100)` on one offer, picked by `nextInt` from the same stream. Rerolls pick the sale again.
- Reroll: costs `2 + rerolls`, with 1-based `n`.
- Sell: `floor(base*version/2)`, where skills and memories count as v1 and starters count as common.

The fork paths `shop/<nodeId>` and `shop/<nodeId>/reroll/<n>` match run-state.md. The discard flow keeps the shop. When the gained item is discarded, the mode becomes `next` ('shop') and the pending becomes `resume`, with the offer still marked sold and the credits paid. When a slot item is discarded, `gain` clears the pending and `?? p.resume` restores the shop. The reward flow is unchanged because `resume` is undefined there and the pending stays null. The Notes decisions are sound and in scope: 1-based reroll index, distinct offers, `lastTool` meaning no equipped tool left, and `prune` left out of the union (vertical-slice: Prune absent). Leaving out the `budget-cuts` +25% is also correct, because lint rules are outside the slice.
