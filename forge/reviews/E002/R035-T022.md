Judgement on the GDD-gap choices in Notes:
- Chain window measured from the chain's first pipe: consistent with event-log.md "within 1000 ms", the `pipe_chain` achievement ("5 pipes within 1000 ms") and juice-audio.md "reset after 1 s". Accepted.
- Prime binding at creation to the matching tool that is predicted to fire soonest: consistent with statuses.md "chip on the tool" and event-log.md `prime` t -> t. The prediction is integer-only and deterministic. Accepted (see F2).
- Consumption at the start of the activation, with a prime made during an activation waiting for the next one: matches "the next activation". Applying the prime to guard and heal matches combat.md ("same formula applies to Guardrails and healing"). Accepted.
- fold.test.ts scope extension (authorised by the orchestrator): the `view.end` assertion now derives its values from the `resolveCombat` result. The fold is still checked against the sim, and `resolvedAt` on every enemy still implies a win, so the test is not weakened. Accepted.
- The `TODO(T033)` in pipes.ts is tracked: T033 lists `pipeMs` mods.
